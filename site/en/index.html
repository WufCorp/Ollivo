Get started

Three steps to the first answer

Everything the model needs, the app downloads and sets up by itself.

Install

The wizard checks your computer and graphics driver and suggests a disk with enough space. The app installs everything else itself — no command line, no administrator rights.

Add a model

Any model you like: drag the file into the window — the app figures out what it is and whether it will run. Models you already downloaded are picked up without copying. Or take a tested one from the picks.

GGUF fileHuggingFaceLM StudioOllamaComfyUI

Talk

GPU layers, conversation memory and the other settings are tuned to your hardware. You just type — or speak into the microphone.

Features

Everything that already works

Only what the app has today. Images, video and voice-over are planned — see “What's next” below.

Chat the way you're used to

The answer appears as it's written; tables and code are formatted, with a “Copy” button. Conversations are saved and searchable. Roles and answer style — “Precise · Balanced · Creative” — instead of cryptic numbers.

Documents

Attach a contract, a report or a book — the model reads it and answers from the text. If a document is longer than the model can hold, the app warns you before sending instead of silently cutting it.

Photos

Show the model a picture and ask what's in it. Phone photos are rotated the right way. If the model can't see images, Ollivo suggests a suitable one that can.

Voice

Dictate your question instead of typing it. Attach a meeting recording or a Telegram voice message — you get the text, and you can ask questions about it.

Sets itself up

How many layers to give the graphics card and how much conversation to keep in memory — the app decides. Not in use for 10 minutes — the model frees the graphics card and wakes up on the next question.

Project folder

Open a folder — the model sees its files, finds what it needs, edits them piece by piece and creates new ones. Any change by the model can be undone with one “Restore” button: copies are kept for 30 days. The model can't run programs.

Errors you can understand

Instead of “CUDA error 700” — what happened in plain words and a button that fixes it: “Run lighter”, “Try again”, “Install components”. Didn't help — “Report a problem”: you see the whole report before sending, and your conversations are not in it.

Honestly

Know how it will run before you download

A model that answers one word a minute isn't a “powerful model” — it's a wasted evening and ten gigabytes on your disk. So every variant in the catalog shows right away whether it will run on your computer.

  • A traffic light for every modelCalculated for your graphics card and memory, not for an average computer.
  • Speed in words, not “tokens”With a comparison: faster than you read, or slower.
  • Compression in one phrase“Almost like the original, at half the size” instead of “Q4_K_M”.

Privacy

Your conversations stay with you

The model runs on your computer. Conversations, documents, photos and recordings aren't sent anywhere — they're processed right here. Ollivo collects no statistics about you. Once the model is downloaded, you can turn the internet off — the chat keeps working.

This site has no trackers or cookies either.

FAQ

The short version

What computer do I need?

Windows and an NVIDIA graphics card. Small models run even on an old card with 4 GB of memory, or on the processor alone — slower. With 8 GB of video memory, capable everyday models already work. The app checks your computer and shows in the catalog what will run on it.

What about AMD, Intel graphics and Mac?

Not yet. First we're polishing one combination — Windows and NVIDIA — so that everything works on it without help. AMD, Intel and macOS come after version 1.0.

Where do I get models?

Anywhere you like. Ollivo works with models in the GGUF format: download any from HuggingFace or elsewhere and drag the file into the window. If you already have LM Studio, Ollama or ComfyUI, the app finds their models itself and uses them without copying — no doubled disk space.

Don't know where to start? The catalog has a small selection of tested models and a HuggingFace search.

Do I need the internet?

Only to download the app, the engine and a model. After that, chat, documents, photos and voice work offline.

How much space does it take?

The app and the engine take tens of megabytes. Models take most of the space: from 1.3 GB for small ones to 15–20 GB for big ones. Speech recognition (~470 MB) downloads only when you first click the microphone. The program folder can be moved to another disk in the settings.

How is this different from LM Studio or Ollama?

They're great, but made for people who know what “quantization” and “context” mean. Ollivo is for everyone else: plain words instead of jargon, a traffic light before you download, errors with a “fix it” button. Plus documents, photos, voice and a project folder in one window — with images, video and voice-over to follow.

Is there a Russian version?

Yes — the app and this site work in English and Russian. The app picks the language of your Windows on first launch; you can change it at any time in the settings.

How much does it cost?

Nothing for now: it's an early version, and your feedback matters more to us. If something doesn't work, click “Report a problem” in the app.
