Ollivo now speaks English.

**What's new**
- English: “Settings → Язык · Language”. The language switches at once, no restart needed.
  On first launch the app picks the language of your Windows; existing users keep Russian.
- Everything is in English: the window, hints and errors, model descriptions in the catalog, the problem report.
  Models also get their instructions in English — so they answer in the language you ask in.
- The website in English: https://wufcorp.github.io/Ollivo/en/

Something wrong? Click “Report” at the bottom left — it really helps.
